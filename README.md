# Gold Price Forecasting

A time-series forecasting project focused on analyzing historical gold prices, comparing statistical forecasting methods, and generating long-term price projections up to 2040.

## Workflow

**1. Data Preparation**

* Prepare historical gold price data covering approximately the last 10 years.
* Consider supporting economic indicators, including crude oil prices and US inflation.
* Organize data into weekly and monthly time-series formats.

**2. Data Preprocessing**

* Check data completeness and consistency.
* Prepare historical observations for time-series analysis and forecasting.

**3. Forecasting Model Development**

Implement three statistical forecasting methods:

* **Simple Moving Average (SMA):** Smooth historical price fluctuations using the average of observations over a specified period.
* **Weighted Moving Average (WMA):** Assign higher weights to recent observations to make forecasts more responsive to recent price movements.
* **ARIMA:** Model historical time-series patterns to generate future gold price projections.

**4. Model Evaluation**

* Evaluate forecasting performance using Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Mean Squared Error (MSE), and Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE).
* Compare forecasting errors and analyze the characteristics, performance, and limitations of each method.

**5. Forecasting and Visualization**

* Generate long-term gold price projections up to 2040 using ARIMA.
* Visualize historical price movements and forecasted trends.
* Interpret the results and identify the limitations of long-term forecasting.

## Methods & Techniques

* **Forecasting:** SMA, WMA, ARIMA
* **Analysis:** Time-Series Analysis, Statistical Modeling, Predictive Analytics
* **Evaluation:** MAE, MSE, MAPE
* **Visualization:** Historical and Forecasted Price Trends

## Objective

To explore and compare statistical forecasting techniques for gold price prediction, evaluate their forecasting errors, and analyze potential long-term price trends based on historical observations.

## Key Findings

SMA achieved a MAPE of 1.91%, while WMA recorded 3.54%. ARIMA generated long-term projections through 2040, illustrating projected price trends while highlighting the limitations and uncertainty of long-term forecasting.
